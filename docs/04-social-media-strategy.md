# 04 - Social Media Strategy, LinkedIn & Instagram Content Plans

## Goals

- Grow awareness among students and graduates and turn it into internship applications.
- Build trust: show real output, mentor review and verifiable certificates.
- Generate service inquiries from small businesses and startups - without paid ads.

## Content pillars (80% value / 20% promotion)

| Pillar | Share | What | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn | 40% | Free value: roadmaps, tips, tools, mini-tutorials | Students, Graduates |
| Proof | 25% | Intern projects, mentor feedback snippets, certificate verification | Students, Graduates |
| Community | 15% | Behind the scenes, polls, Q&A, intern spotlights | All |
| Programs | 10% | Tracks, levels, batch dates (1st & 15th) | Students, Graduates |
| Services | 10% | Web/app/cloud/data/marketing for businesses | Small businesses, Service customers |

## Platform plan

| Platform | Role | Frequency | Formats | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Primary - graduates, recruiters, startup founders | 4 posts/week | Carousels, text + image, polls, short video | Credibility and B2B leads |
| Instagram | Primary - students | 4 posts/week + daily stories | Reels (30-45 s), carousels, stories, highlights | Reach and applications |
| Facebook | Secondary - local small businesses | 3 posts/week | Image posts, short videos, local groups | Service inquiries |
| Google Business Profile | Local search | 1 update/week | Updates, photos, Q&A | Local discovery |

## LinkedIn content plan

| Week | Posts |
|---|---|
| Week 1 - Why Veda | Launch post on the 'output, not attendance' promise; carousel: How Level 1-2-3 works; poll on biggest internship worry; founder/mentor intro post. |
| Week 2 - Learn | Carousel: 6-week Web Dev roadmap; text post: what recruiters check in an internship; doc post: portfolio checklist (lead magnet); intern project spotlight. |
| Week 3 - Proof | How to verify a certificate (step carousel); mentor feedback example (anonymised); 'day in the life' of a virtual intern; services post: one team for web, app, cloud. |
| Week 4 - Convert | Batch reminder (offer letters on 1st/15th); FAQ carousel; business post: 5 signs your business needs a website; recap + thank-you post with apply link. |

**Rules**

- Company page posts + reshares by mentors/team (employee advocacy)
- Encourage interns to tag Veda in their project posts and reshare the best ones
- Hook in the first 2 lines, one CTA per post, link in first comment
- Post Tue-Thu, 9-11 am IST; reply to every comment within 24 h

## Instagram content plan

| Week | Posts |
|---|---|
| Week 1 - Awareness | Reel: 'Free internship? Here's how to spot a real one'; carousel: tracks at a glance; story poll: which track? |
| Week 2 - Learn | Reel: build a landing page in 60 s; carousel: Python basics cheat sheet; story Q&A with a mentor. |
| Week 3 - Proof | Reel: intern project showcase; carousel: certificate verification steps; stories: daily task streaks. |
| Week 4 - Convert | Reel: apply in 2 minutes walkthrough; carousel: FAQ; story countdown to the next batch date. |

**Rules**

- Highlights: Apply, Tracks, Certificates, Services, FAQ
- Bio link -> apply page with UTM (utm_source=instagram)
- Navy + orange Canva templates; captions under 125 words
- 3-5 targeted hashtags e.g. #virtualinternship #btechstudents #webdevelopment

## Branding

Navy (`#0b1e4d`) backgrounds with orange (`#f97316`) highlights, white text, one Canva template family per format (reel cover, carousel, quote card).
