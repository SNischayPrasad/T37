# 01 - Audience Analysis, Personas & Service-wise Mapping

## Veda Technology at a glance (public information only)

- Offers free (no application fee), 100% virtual internships that generally run 4-6 weeks.
- Targets B.Tech, BCA, MCA, M.Tech, diploma students and graduates.
- Internship tracks: Web Development, Data Science, Data Analytics, Python, Java, Android, Cloud Computing, Graphic Design and UI/UX.
- Task-based learning in Level 1, 2 and 3 tracks, so beginners and advanced learners both progress.
- Dedicated mentor review on every submitted task; attendance is marked by daily task submission.
- Internship and completion certificate with a unique verification ID; Letter of Recommendation based on performance.
- Offer letters are processed on the 1st and 15th of each month; one internship per month per applicant.
- IT services for businesses: Web Development, App Development, Cloud Computing, Data Science and Digital Marketing.
- Registered under the MSME/Udyam framework. Based in Lakhisarai, Bihar. Contact: support@vedatechnology.in.
- Brand colours: navy (site theme colour #0b1e4d) with orange accents.

**Positioning:** Most internship certificates prove attendance - Veda Technology's prove output. The campaign builds on the brand's own promise: structured levels, mentor-reviewed tasks and verifiable certificates for learners, and one multi-skill team for businesses.

## Target audience personas

### Rahul - the 2nd-year B.Tech student

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Segment | Students |
| Profile | 19, B.Tech CSE/AI at a tier-3 college, first smartphone-first learner, limited budget. |
| Goals | Get a first internship certificate, build 2-3 projects for GitHub, learn a skill beyond the syllabus. |
| Pain points | Has seen 'pay Rs. 999 for certificate' scams; worried about exams clashing; unsure where to start. |
| Where they are | Instagram reels, YouTube tutorials, college WhatsApp groups, just started LinkedIn. |
| Content that works | Beginner roadmaps, '1 project a week' reels, myth-busting about virtual internships. |
| Primary CTA | Apply free for a Level 1 track |

### Priya - the fresh graduate

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Segment | Graduates |
| Profile | 22, BCA graduate (2026), applying for fresher roles, active on LinkedIn and Naukri. |
| Goals | Show recruiters real, reviewed work; earn an LOR; move from Level 1 to Level 3 depth. |
| Pain points | Every job asks for experience; certificates without proof are ignored by recruiters. |
| Where they are | LinkedIn feed and carousels, Telegram job channels, email. |
| Content that works | Resume and portfolio tips, intern project showcases, 'what recruiters check' posts. |

| Field | Detail |
