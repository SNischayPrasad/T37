# 06 - Lead-Generation Campaign & Call-to-Action Strategy

## Campaign concept

- **Learners:** *Build. Ship. Get Verified.* - learn, ship real projects, earn a verifiable certificate.
- **Businesses:** *Go Digital with Veda* - one team for web, app, cloud, data and marketing.
- **Budget:** Rs. 0 on ads. Organic content, SEO, community and intern advocacy only.

## Funnel

| Stage | Tactics | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reels, carousels, SEO blog posts, Google Business Profile | Reach, impressions, new followers |
| Interest | Roadmaps, cheat sheets, mentor Q&A | Engagement rate, saves, profile visits |
| Consideration | Track pages, FAQ, certificate verification, project showcases | Website visits, time on page |
| Conversion | Apply form (learners); quote form / WhatsApp (businesses) | Applications, inquiries, conversion rate |
| Advocacy | Interns tag Veda in project posts; reshares; testimonials | Mentions, user-generated posts |

## Lead magnets

| Lead magnet | Audience | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internship-ready portfolio checklist (PDF) | Students & graduates | LinkedIn document post / Google Form | Ready to use |
| 6-week Web Development roadmap (PDF) | Students | Instagram + LinkedIn | Ready to use |
| Free website quote with a short consultation | Small businesses | Facebook, Google Business Profile | Confirm with Veda team before launch |

## Lead capture & nurture

Capture via the website apply form, a Google Form for lead magnets, and WhatsApp/email for businesses.

- Day 0: thank-you message + the promised PDF + link to track pages
- Day 2: 'Which track suits you?' guide + certificate verification explainer
- Day 5: batch reminder (offer letters on the 1st and 15th) + apply link
- Businesses: reply within 24 h on WhatsApp/email, share process and next steps

## Call-to-action strategy

| Funnel stage | Audience | CTA | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Students | Save this for later / Follow for daily tips | End of reels and carousels |
| Interest | Students & graduates | Download the free checklist | Document posts, story link sticker |
| Consideration | Graduates | See how certificate verification works | Certificate carousel, website |
| Conversion | Students & graduates | Apply free - next batch on the 1st/15th | Bio link, post first comment, website header |
| Conversion | Small businesses | Request a free quote | Facebook posts, service pages, GBP |
| Conversion | Service customers | Book a discovery call / request a quote | LinkedIn services posts, service pages |
| Advocacy | Interns | Tag @Veda Technology in your project post | Dashboard reminder, completion message |

**CTA rules**

- One primary CTA per post
- Start with a verb (Apply, Download, Request, Save)
- Give value first; promotion in at most 2 of every 10 posts
- Every link carries a UTM tag so conversions are attributed to the right post

**UTM example:** `https://vedatechnology.in/apply.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=build_ship_verified&utm_content=day09_roadmap`
