# 02 - Competitor Analysis & Keyword Research

## Competitor landscape

> Qualitative comparison by competitor *type*, based on publicly visible positioning. No claims are made about any specific competitor's numbers.

| Competitor type | Examples | Strengths | Weaknesses | Veda Technology's angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Internship marketplaces | e.g. Internshala | Huge listing volume, strong brand trust, SEO dominance | Generic listings; quality varies by employer; little structured learning | Structured Level 1-3 tracks with mentor review on every task |
| Other free virtual-internship providers | Many task-based programs popular on student LinkedIn | Viral reach through intern LinkedIn posts and tagging | Often seen as 'certificate mills'; weak verification | Lead with proof: verification ID, mentor review, output-based certificates |
| Paid ed-tech courses and training institutes | Bootcamps, coaching institutes | Big brands, placement messaging, structured syllabus | High fees; long duration | Free to apply and 4-6 weeks - lower risk to try |
| Local web agencies and freelancers (services) | Local developers, freelance marketplaces | Low price, local relationships | Inconsistent quality; single skill; poor follow-up | One team covering web, app, cloud, data and marketing |

## SWOT - Veda Technology

**Strengths**

- Free, 100% virtual, low-risk offer
- Wide set of tech tracks
- Level-wise structure + mentor review
- Verifiable certificates; MSME/Udyam registered

**Weaknesses**

- Low brand awareness vs. marketplaces
- Few public case studies for services
- Homepage stats show '0+' to crawlers/no-JS visitors
- Service links point to one #services section

**Opportunities**

- Intern-generated content (every intern posts on LinkedIn)
- Long-tail SEO for each track
- Local SEO for Bihar/tier-2 businesses
- Hindi + English content for SMBs

**Threats**

- Distrust of free internships due to scams
- Large competitors outranking on broad keywords
- Algorithm changes reducing organic reach
