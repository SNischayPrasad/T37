## 📌 Overview

A complete, realistic digital marketing campaign plan for **Veda Technology** - covering its virtual internship programs (Web Development, Data Science, Data Analytics, Python, Java, Android, Cloud Computing, Graphic Design, UI/UX) and its IT services for businesses (Web Development, App Development, Cloud Computing, Data Science, Digital Marketing).

The plan analyses the target audience, builds a content strategy, improves online visibility, designs organic campaigns and estimates expected performance - **with zero spend on paid advertising** and **only publicly available Veda Technology information**.

## 🎯 Objective

Gain practical experience in planning and managing digital marketing for a real technology and service-oriented brand, and understand how **SEO, social media, content marketing, lead generation and website promotion** work together.

## 💡 Core idea

> Most internship certificates prove attendance - Veda Technology's prove output. The campaign builds on the brand's own promise: structured levels, mentor-reviewed tasks and verifiable certificates for learners, and one multi-skill team for businesses.

## 🧱 Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Learn | 40% | Free value: roadmaps, tips, tools, mini-tutorials |
| Proof | 25% | Intern projects, mentor feedback snippets, certificate verification |
| Community | 15% | Behind the scenes, polls, Q&A, intern spotlights |

| Services | 10% | Web/app/cloud/data/marketing for businesses |

**80% value, 20% promotion** - most posts teach or prove something; only Programs and Services posts promote.

## 🔑 Key insights

1. **Trust converts.** For a free internship brand, proof (mentor review, verification IDs, real projects) matters more than promotion.
2. **Every intern is a channel.** Interns already post on LinkedIn - a simple "tag Veda in your project post" habit is free, credible reach.
3. **One page, one CTA.** SEO, social and lead magnets should all point to the same track or service page with UTM tracking.
4. **Small website fixes, big impact.** The homepage counters read "0+" in the page text before JavaScript runs, and all services share one `#services` anchor - fixing both helps SEO and first impressions.
